🧪 Facilitator Hook: The 30-Second Pitch Comparison

Read both elevator pitch samples below. Which story makes you actually want to hire or partner with this student?

Sample A (Pure AI Generator) Generic AI

"I am a highly motivated, results-oriented computer science major passionate about leveraging cutting-edge synergies to drive high-impact solutions. I spearheaded a multifaceted project optimizing cross-functional workflow efficiency, serving as a testament to my dedication and transformative innovation."

Sample B (Human-Centered Purpose) Human Core

"When our student team's campus tutoring database crashed two days before midterms, I saw how stressed freshman students were getting. I spent the night restructuring our database queries—not just to fix the code, but because I cared about keeping tutoring accessible when my peers needed it most."

✅ Insights Unlocked!

Why Sample B Wins: It demonstrates Purpose (helping stressed peers), Struggle (database crash under time pressure), and Human Problem Solving. Sample A uses corporate AI buzzwords ("synergies", "spearheaded", "testament") that mask your real human effort.

Human First, AI Second

Never ask AI to write from scratch. Supply your raw human P-S-I facts first.

Rule #2

Don't Delegate Your Voice

Use AI to structure and ask questions, not to swap in generic corporate buzzwords.

Rule #3

Audit for Inclusion & Truth

Verify that your story is clear to non-experts and free from artificial embellishments.
